```markdown
![The Hatman: Hollow House](BANNER_LINKI)

> *"This is Detective Ruth Calloway. If you're hearing this, I've been inside Marlowe House longer than I planned...
> Whatever he asks you — do not lie to him."*

**The Hatman: Hollow House** is a story-driven analog horror mod. You are a detective. Your partner walked into Marlowe House nineteen days ago and left you one voicemail. You go in after her.

Something very tall is already inside. It wears a hat. It knocks before it enters. It asks permission. It is unfailingly polite — and it does not forgive a lie.

## What's inside

- **A full story in five chapters** — about 45–60 minutes. Chapters unlock in order; no skipping ahead.
- **The Hatman** — a GeckoLib-animated stalker with his own AI: he freezes while you watch him and moves when you look away, comes up through the floor, drops from the ceiling, cuts you off on the stairs, ducks under doorways, crawls through tunnels, and searches the wardrobes you hide in. The more you hide, the more he checks.
- **Branching conversations** — he talks to you through doors, on a dead telephone, across a table. Lies and insults make him angry (and the house darker). Agreeing to everything makes you *his*.
- **Five endings**, one of them secret. How you spoke to him all night decides which one you get.
- **Night-vision camcorder** — sees in the dark and sees *him*. Runs on batteries you have to find.
- **Chases and hiding** — barred doors, wardrobes, cellars, a long run from the attic to the cold room.
- **Cutscenes** with camera work and subtitles, chapter cards, a VHS look.
- **25 notes and documents** — hidden in desks, drawers, cabinets and trunks, never just lying on the floor. Piece together what the hatter Edmund Marlowe did in 1962.
- **Marlowe House** — three floors, a cellar, an attic and grounds, built around you with one command.
- **English and Türkçe.** The story follows your game language.

## How to play

1. Install **Forge** and **GeckoLib** for your Minecraft version.
2. Put the mod in your `mods` folder.
3. Create a world, stand somewhere open and type **`/hatman start`**.
4. Press **V** for the camera. Read everything. Answer carefully.

> ⚠️ The house is built where you stand and clears an area of about 70 × 75 blocks. Use a new world.

**Commands:** `/hatman start` · `/hatman stop` · `/hatman status` (Turkish aliases: `basla`, `bitir`, `durum`)
**Singleplayer story.**
```
