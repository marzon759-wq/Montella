## Description

```
Whatever he asks you — do not lie to him.

The Hatman: Hollow House is a story-driven analog horror mod for Minecraft (Forge 1.20.1 and 1.21.1).
You are a detective. Your partner, Ruth Calloway, walked into Marlowe House nineteen days ago and left you a single voicemail. You go in after her.

Something very tall is already inside. It wears a hat. It knocks before it enters. It asks permission. It is unfailingly polite — and it never forgives a lie.

This video shows about five minutes of gameplay from the full story, all the way to one of the five endings.

WHAT'S IN THE MOD
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• A full story in five chapters with cutscenes and subtitles
• The Hatman — a GeckoLib-animated stalker with his own AI: he freezes when you look at him, moves when you look away, comes up through the floor, cuts you off on the stairs and searches the wardrobes you hide in
• Branching conversations — he remembers how you spoke to him
• Five endings, one of them secret
• A night-vision camcorder that sees what you can't
• Chases, hiding, barred doors
• 25 notes hidden in desks, drawers and trunks
• Marlowe House: three floors, a cellar, an attic and grounds, built with one command
• English and Turkish

HOW TO PLAY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. Install Forge and GeckoLib for your Minecraft version
2. Put the mod in your mods folder
3. Create a new world and type /hatman start
4. Press V for the camera
```
