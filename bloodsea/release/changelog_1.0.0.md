## Blood Sea 1.0.0 - first release

- The full story: 18 cutscenes, timed choices, four endings
- Pip the cat: a cat AI with many animations; she brings you fish
- Survival: hunger, drifting cargo crates, blood that kills
- A real night under the Blood Moon, with a countdown to first light
- The Watcher: the white sun that opens into an eye
- Five creatures, omens at sea, an original synthesised score
- Its own title screen
- English and Turkish
