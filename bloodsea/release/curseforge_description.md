# Blood Sea

**You wake face down on a raft in an endless sea of blood.** There is a black cat you have never seen, a hand-drawn map, and a white sun that is not quite a sun. Why are you here? Where is this?

*Blood Sea* is a story-driven horror survival mod. Create a new world and the story begins by itself.

---

## What's inside

- **A story with 18 cutscenes.** Fully staged scenes with dialogue that types itself out, a letterboxed camera and an original score. The opening is in third person. Everything else you play in first person.
- **Choices that matter.** Timed choices weigh *hope*, *acceptance*, *courage* and *fury*. Whichever weighs most decides which of **four endings** you reach.
- **Pip.** A black cat with dozens of animations. She sleeps on the blanket, grooms, stretches, rubs against you, hisses at what is in the water and hooks fish out of the blood for you. The more you care for her, the more she brings. You will get attached.
- **Survival.** You get hungry. Pull drifting cargo crates aboard, eat what Pip brings, and never touch the blood: it kills in seconds.
- **A real night.** The sun sets and the **Blood Moon** rises on about six and a half minutes of darkness, with a countdown to first light.
  - Something follows the raft, so keep rowing.
  - Something in the water hunts by sound, so crouch and keep still.
  - The lantern on its pole is your only light.
- **The Watcher.** The sun is a plain white disc until the story opens it. A crack splits it and it parts like an eyelid. After that it opens again at the worst moments.
- **Five creatures that cannot be killed:**
  - the Wake Follower
  - the Bone Gatherer, which assembles out of the blood
  - the blind Name Caller
  - the Changer, a barrel that is not a barrel
  - the Leviathan of the eastern deep
- **Omens.** Between chapters the sea does things it never explains:
  - a ship's bell far off
  - blood falling as rain
  - a moan from below
  - a woman's whisper behind you
  - lanterns on the horizon that go out when you look at them
  - another raft with a man like you standing on it, watching
- **A living sea.** The swell is a real moving mesh and the raft is a vehicle you steer with the oar.
- **Its own title screen**, and an original synthesised score: *drift* by day, *night*, and *dread* when the eye is open or something is close.
- **Languages:** English and Turkish. Other languages fall back to English.

## How to play

1. Create a **new singleplayer world**. The story starts by itself after a few seconds.
2. **Right-click** the steering oar at the back of the raft to sit, then row with **W/A/S/D**.
3. **Right-click** Pip to stroke her, and right-click floating crates to pull them aboard.
4. **Right-click** with the map to see where you are and where you are heading.
5. **Hold SPACE** to skip a cutscene.

If you die, you wake on the raft again. Your things stay with you.

## Türkçe

**Sonsuz bir kan denizinde, bir salın üstünde yüzüstü uyanıyorsun.** Yanında daha önce hiç görmediğin siyah bir kedi, elle çizilmiş bir harita ve tam olarak güneş olmayan beyaz bir güneş var. Neden buradasın? Burası neresi?

*Blood Sea*, hikâye odaklı bir korku ve hayatta kalma modu. Yeni bir dünya oluştur; hikâye kendiliğinden başlar.

- **18 ara sahneli bir hikâye.** Diyaloglar harf harf yazılıyor ve oyuna özel müzikler eşlik ediyor.
- **Önemli seçimler.** Süreli seçimlerin dört sondan hangisine ulaşacağını belirliyor.
- **Pip.** Onlarca animasyonu olan, sana kandan balık getiren siyah bir kedi.
- **Hayatta kalma.** Açlık var. Yüzen sandıkları topla ve kana asla dokunma.
- **Gerçek bir gece.** Kanlı Ay altında yaklaşık 6,5 dakikalık bir gece ve şafağa geri sayım var.
  - Seni takip eden bir şey var; kürek çekmeye devam et.
  - Sesle avlanan bir şey var; eğil ve kıpırdama.
- **Gözcü.** Beyaz güneş bir gün göz kapağı gibi açılıyor.
- **Öldürülemeyen beş yaratık**, denizde açıklanamayan **kehanetler** ve modun **kendine özel ana menüsü** var.
