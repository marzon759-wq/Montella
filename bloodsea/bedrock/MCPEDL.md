# MCPEDL page: Blood Sea (Bedrock add-on)

## Title
```
Blood Sea: Adrift - Story Horror Survival Add-On
```

## Short description
```
Wake on a raft in an endless sea of blood. A story-driven horror survival add-on with 18 cutscenes, choices, four endings, a black cat named Pip and five creatures that cannot be killed.
```

## Description
```
You wake face down on a raft in an endless sea of blood. There is a black cat you have never seen, a hand-drawn map, and a white sun that is not quite a sun. Why are you here? Where is this?

Blood Sea is a story-driven horror survival add-on. Make a new world with the add-on, and the story begins by itself.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- A story told in 18 cutscenes, with a moving camera and dialogue (English and Turkish).
- Choices that matter: what you choose weighs hope, acceptance, courage or fury, and the heaviest decides which of FOUR ENDINGS you reach.
- Pip, a black cat with 20 animations. She sleeps on the blanket, grooms, stretches, rubs against you, hisses at what is in the water and fishes for you. The more you care for her, the more she brings.
- Survival: you get hungry. Pull drifting cargo crates aboard, eat what Pip brings, and never touch the blood. It kills you.
- A real night under the Blood Moon, about six and a half minutes long, with a countdown to first light. Keep rowing if something follows you. Crouch and keep still if something listens.
- The Watcher: the sun is a white disc until the story opens it. Then it splits like an eyelid and looks back. Don't stare at it.
- Five creatures that cannot be killed: the Wake Follower, the Bone Gatherer, the blind Name Caller, the Changer (a barrel that is not a barrel) and the Leviathan.
- Omens between chapters: a ship's bell far off, blood rain, a moan from below, a whisper behind you, lanterns that go out when you look at them, and another raft with a man like you on it, watching.
- Four regions to row through: the Bone Reach, Leviathan's Deep, the Mirage and the Edge of the World.
- An original score: drift by day, night after dark, and dread when something is close.
- Its own title screen: a blood-red panorama, the dripping logo and a new splash text. For this, turn on the resource pack under Settings > Global Resources.

HOW TO PLAY (made for touch screens)
Your hotbar holds four controls:
- Oar (slot 1): tap it to sit at the oar or stand up. Row with the joystick; you steer where you look.
- Map (slot 2): tap it to see where you are and which way to go.
- Call Pip (slot 3): tap it and she comes to you. Tap again when she is close to stroke her.
- Skip scene (slot 9): tap it during a cutscene to skip it.
You can also tap Pip to stroke her (holding food feeds her) and tap a floating crate to pull it aboard. On PC, right-click does the same, and holding crouch also skips a scene.

Made by krolasyon. Title screen splash: "by Kyvix Photo Editor" (Google Play: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kyvix.photoeditor)

Cat sounds: "Cat purr & meow" by kerzoven (CC0), "Meow" by ignasd (CC0), "Cat SFX" by macro (CC-BY 3.0), all from freesound.org. The music and all other sounds are original.
```

## Installation
```
1. Download BloodSea_Bedrock.mcaddon and open it. Minecraft imports both packs.
2. Create a NEW world. Under Add-Ons, turn on "Blood Sea (Behaviour)". The resource pack is added with it.
3. Start the world. The story begins by itself after a few seconds.
4. Optional: to get the Blood Sea title screen, turn on "Blood Sea (Resources)" under Settings > Global Resources.

Recommended: Normal difficulty, sound on, render distance 8 or more.
```

## Supported versions
```
Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.0 and newer (Android, iOS, Windows, consoles). No experimental toggles needed.
```
