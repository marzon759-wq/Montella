# The Hatman: Hollow House — credits

Story, house, models, animation and code: krolasyon & Claude.

## Sound effects

All sound effects are real recordings released under **CC0 (public domain)** on [OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org),
cut, layered and mastered by `tools/gen_sounds.py`. Nothing is synthesised.

| Used for | Source (OpenGameArt) | Author |
