## Sound effects (2.1)

The battle and special move sounds rebuilt in 2.1 are real recordings released under **CC0 (public domain)** on
[OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org), cut, layered and mastered by `tools/gen_sfx.py`.
No audio from the anime or any other copyrighted source is used.

## Your own sounds

Every sound can be replaced with a resource pack: put an `.ogg` with the same name under
`assets/librabeyblade/sounds/` (for example `clash1.ogg`, `crater.ogg`, `tornado.ogg`, `meteor_dive.ogg`,
`pegasus_neigh.ogg`). The names are listed in `assets/librabeyblade/sounds.json`.
