## Beyblade Metal Fight 2.3.0

**Requires GeckoLib** (1.20.1: GeckoLib 4.4+, 1.21.1: GeckoLib 4.7+).

### New
- **Fully animated with GeckoLib**: spin, precession wobble, lean into turns, recoil on every hit, launch and landing,
  burst finish (Face Bolt, Energy Ring, Fusion Wheel, Spin Track and Tip fly apart) and sleep-outs.
- **3D L-Drago dragon**: a real 3D Eastern dragon coils around L-Drago, strikes at the opponent and breathes fire.
- **Clash wind**: hits blast wind out sideways, throw up a dust ring and flash both beys' energies.
- **Special moves hit the world**: craters, tornadoes, shock waves, magma and sand. Blocks repair themselves after
  about 30 seconds (`/gamerule beybladeWorldDamage false` turns block changes off).
- Star Blast Attack now launches Pegasus into the sky for a shooting star dive; Storm Bringer is now a tornado.
- **14 new Performance Tips** (HF, WF, WSF, RS, RB, RDF, LRF, SD, DS, MS, MB, GB, Q, B:D) and
  **9 new Spin Tracks** (120, 130, 160, CH120, ED145, DF145, AD145, SW145, LW160).
- **Tip physics**: rubber grips and wears, sharp holds the centre, ball recovers, free spinning tips lose less spin,
  metal slides and sparks, Quake bounces.
- Screen shake, field of view punch and impact flash.
- New sound effects (clashes, craters, tornadoes, wind, sky launch, meteor dive).
- **Now also for Minecraft 1.21.1.**

### Changed
- Special moves take longer to build up: 2.5x cooldowns, no special moves in the first 10 seconds of a battle,
  rival beys wait at least 20 seconds between moves. Shields and awakenings last 1.5x longer.
- Pop-up texts removed (action bar, chat, titles): the battle shows itself through effects and sound.
