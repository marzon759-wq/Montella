# Beyblade Metal Fight

**Build your bey, rip it into the stadium and feel the battle.** Metal Fight style beyblades for Minecraft:
Face Bolt, Energy Ring, Fusion Wheel, Spin Track and Performance Tip. Every bey has its own special moves, its own spirit
and its own way of fighting, and every special move hits the world around it.

Fully animated with **GeckoLib**: spin, precession wobble, recoil on every hit, burst finishes, sleep-outs and special moves.

## 🐉 Spirits and special moves

- **L-Drago's dragon is a real 3D creature**: a long Eastern dragon with scales, dorsal fins, four clawed legs, horns,
  glowing eyes, whiskers and a jaw that opens to roar. It coils around L-Drago, rears up, strikes at the opponent and
  breathes fire with *Dragon Emperor Life Destructor*.
- **Pegasus Star Blast Attack**: Pegasus is hurled into the sky, its spirit spreads its wings above the target, and it comes down
  like a shooting star.
- **Storm Bringer**: Pegasus races around its opponent and whips up a tornado that lifts it into the air and drops it.
- **Inferno Blast**: Libra drills into the ground, the earth turns to sand and erupts in a burning sand tornado.
- Special moves take time to build up, like in the anime: long cooldowns, none in the opening seconds of a battle.

## 🌪️ The battle hits the world

- **Clash wind**: when two beys slam together, wind blasts out sideways, a dust ring races across the floor, both energies
  flash at the point of contact, light mobs and items get blown away and grass is laid flat.
- **Craters, tornadoes and shock waves**: dives leave craters (burning magma for L-Drago, sand for Libra, scorched earth
  for Pegasus), tornadoes tear up grass, flowers and leaves and carry mobs, shock waves shatter glass.
- **Everything repairs itself**: changed blocks come back on their own after about 30 seconds, and all of it is put back
  when the world closes. Chests and other block entities are never touched.
  Turn the block changes off with `/gamerule beybladeWorldDamage false`.
- Screen shake and a field of view punch on heavy hits, a white flash on big impacts. No pop-up texts: the battle speaks
  for itself.

## 🔧 Build your own bey

Take beys apart and rebuild them at the **Beyblade Workbench**: 12 Face Bolts, 12 Energy Rings, 21 Fusion Wheels,
24 Spin Tracks and **31 Performance Tips**, each with its own stats and weight.

The tip decides how your bey moves:

| Tips | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Rubber: RF, R2F, RSF, LRF, RS, RB, RDF, CS | grips the floor, fastest flower-pattern attack, burns stamina; the rubber wears as the bey slows. LRF is faster on a left spinner |
| Flat: F, XF, HF, WF, MF | aggressive movement around the bowl |
| Sharp: S, ES, CS, DS, MS, B:D | stays in the centre, best stamina |
| Ball: B, WB, RB, MB, GB | absorbs hits, recovers from tilting |
| Defense: D, WD, EWD, SD, DS, RDF | hard to knock out |
| Free spinning: ES, EWD, FD, FS, B:D | the tip turns on its own: loses less spin to hits |
| Metal: MF, MS, MB | slides fast and throws sparks |
| Q (Quake) | bounces around unpredictably |

Spin Tracks include 85 to 230, BD145, C145, W105, UW145, T125, D125, R145, TH170, CH120, ED145, DF145, AD145, SW145 and LW160.

## 🏟️ Stadium, matches and levels

- Place a **Beyblade Stadium**, or find one in the world with a loot chest full of parts.
- Rip your bey with its **launcher** (hold to charge the launch power), give it orders, switch its battle mode
  (Attack / Defense / Stamina / Balance) and unleash its special moves.
- Ride the stadium rail for an **Xtreme Dash**, clash special moves head on, burst or sleep out your opponent.
- Beys gain XP and level up to 30, unlocking stronger moves.
