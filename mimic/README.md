# MIMIC: Harrow Farm

Story-driven horror mod for Minecraft Forge **1.20.1** and **1.21.1** (requires GeckoLib).

Lily called from the family farm: *"It's not Dad anymore. It talks like him."* You drive out to Harrow Farm at three in the
morning. Something with six arms and a skull for a face lives in the barn. It hunts by sound, and it learns every word you say.

- Five chapters, about 40–60 minutes, with Until Dawn style timed decisions and four endings
- A two-storey farmhouse, a red barn with a hayloft, a silo, a butcher's shed and a corn field, built with one command
- 135 custom 3D props: furniture, chandeliers, stairs, carcasses on hooks, scarecrows of bone and antler
- Flashlight with real light (and the thing sees the beam), batteries, hiding in wardrobes and hay, holding your breath
- The creature: GeckoLib model with 32 animations; it climbs, crawls, hangs from the rafters, drags, eats and imitates

**Play:** new world → stand somewhere open → `/mimic start`. **F** toggles the flashlight. Crouch to be quiet.

Build: `tools/build_assets.sh`, then `gradle build` in `forge-1.20.1` and `forge-1.21.1`.
