# MIMIC: Harrow Farm — credits

Story, farm, creature, props, animation and code: krolasyon & Claude.

## Voices
Spoken lines are synthesised with **Kokoro** (Apache-2.0, hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, ONNX build by thewh1teagle) and then treated
(walkie-talkie, telephone, tape, and the mimic's distortion) by `tools/gen_sounds.py`.

## Sound effects
CC0 (public domain) recordings from [OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org), cut and layered by `tools/gen_sounds.py`:
Storm Arwen 2022 (wind), Wind whoosh loop, Car engine start 01 / start up 02, Fire crackling, Fireplace loop,
Squish sound effects, Deep bone crack/break SFX, 7 eating crunches, Crickets ambient loop —
plus the CC0 set already cut for *The Hatman: Hollow House* (see ../CREDITS.md).
