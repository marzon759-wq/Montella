# MIMIC: Harrow Farm

> *"Sam? It's Lily. I know it's late. I know you said never to call. Something's wrong with Dad. It's not Dad anymore. It talks like him. It laughs like him."*

Three weeks after that voicemail, you finally drive home to **Harrow Farm**. It is 3:12 in the morning. Every clock in the house has stopped. Your father's voice answers the phone, and your father is dead.

Something came up out of the root cellar in July. It has six long arms, a skull for a face and a mouth that opens too wide. **It hunts by sound, and it learns every word you say.** Then it uses those words against you.

Find your sister. Find the keys. Get to the truck. And whatever you hear in the house tonight... **don't go upstairs.**

---

## ✦ What is in it

**A real story, start to finish**
- 5 chapters: *Homecoming, The Barn, The Butcher's Shed, The Silo, Dawn*. About 40–60 minutes.
- **Until Dawn-style decisions** with a clock running out: answer the phone or let it ring, whisper or shout, run for the ladder or hide in the hay, drive or go back into the corn, **face it or run**. Silence is a choice too.
- **4 different endings**: *Dawn, The Harvest, Passenger, Learned*. Your choices and the words you said out loud decide which one you get.
- The creature **remembers what you said**. Shout on the radio and it will use your shout later. Say the password out loud and it will know the password.
- Chapters unlock in order. You cannot skip ahead by grabbing the next chapter's items early.

**Fully voiced**
- Every line is voiced: your sister Lily on the walkie-talkie, Dad's last tape, the answering machine, your own inner voice, and the mimic wearing all of their voices. It sounds just a little too slow and a little too low.
- Subtitles in **English and Turkish**.

**A farm built around you**
- One command builds the whole place: a two-storey farmhouse with cellar, a red barn with hayloft, stalls, workshop and slaughter room, a steel silo, Dad's butcher shed, a corn field, scarecrows of bone and antler, and a chained gate.
- **135 custom 3D props**: chandeliers, cast-iron stove, grandfather clock, wardrobes, carcasses on hooks, hay bales, oil lamps, and more.
- Most things are usable. Drawers, chests, trunks and cabinets can be searched. The phone, the stove, the sink, the radio, the well, the bell and the mirror all react. There are **15 notes** to find that tell the family's story.
- **3D items in your hand**: flashlight, walkie-talkie, crowbar, bolt cutters, gas can, Dad's lighter, the pitchfork.

**Survival horror gameplay**
- **Flashlight with real light**, and batteries that run out. The creature sees the beam.
- **Hide** in wardrobes and hay piles and **hold your breath** when it leans in.
- **Stamina**: running takes your breath. Out of breath you can't run, and you pant. It can hear that.
- **Sound matters**: crouch to move quietly. Sprinting, kicking a bucket or ringing the bell will bring it to you.
- **Fight back**: in the final chapter you can take Dad's pitchfork and face it. Dodge its lunge, strike when it misses, and break free when it grabs you.
- Quick-time events, checkpoints, cinematic cutscenes, camera sway when you run, and an interaction prompt under the crosshair so you always know what can be used.

**The creature**
- A custom GeckoLib model with **33 animations**. It walks, stalks, crawls, climbs walls, hangs from the rafters, drags its prey, eats, sniffs, listens, roars, staggers when struck, and talks with a stolen voice.

## ✦ How to play
1. Install **Minecraft Forge** and **GeckoLib** (required, see below).
2. Create a **new world**. A flat or open area works best.
3. Stand somewhere open and type **`/mimic start`**. The farm is built around you and the story begins.
4. **F** toggles the flashlight. **Crouch** to move quietly. **Right-click** to use things. **Space** holds your breath when hiding.

Other commands: `/mimic stop` ends the story, `/mimic status` shows where you are.

> 🎧 **Play at night, with headphones.** The mimic is mostly heard before it is seen.

## ✦ FAQ
**Is it scary?** It is a horror mod with jump scares, gore and a creature that chases you. Not for young children.

**Can I play it in an existing world?** Yes, but it builds a large farm (about 110 × 110 blocks) around you. A new world is recommended.

**Does it work in multiplayer?** The story is made for one player. Others can join and watch, but only one player plays the story.

**The game is too dark!** That is on purpose. Use the flashlight and keep spare batteries. Turning brightness up a little in the video settings is fine.

**I am stuck.** Read the objective in the top-left corner. Look for the white "Right-click: …" prompt under the crosshair. Notes and Lily's radio calls tell you where to go next.
